Alpha
Alpha in mutual funds measures excess return relative to a benchmark, indicating a manager's skill at generating returns beyond market performance.
Sharpe
Sharpe Ratio evaluates a fund's return relative to its risk. Higher values signify better risk-adjusted performance.
Beta
Beta in mutual funds indicates a fund's sensitivity to market changes. A beta over 1 means it's more volatile than the market, while below 1 implies less volatility.
Standard deviation
Standard deviation measures the volatility of a fund's returns. The higher the value, the riskier the investment.

About HDFC Large Cap Fund

HDFC Large Cap Fund is a Large Cap mutual fund scheme from Hdfc Mutual Fund. This fund has been in existence for 29 yrs 8 m, having been launched on 19/08/1996. HDFC Large Cap Fund has ₹35,458 Crores worth of assets under management (AUM) as on 31/03/2026 and is medium-sized fund of its category. The latest Net Asset Value (NAV) of HDFC Large Cap Fund as of 08/05/2026 is ₹1109.14. The fund has an expense ratio of 1.9%, which is higher than what most other Large Cap funds charge.
  • HDFC Large Cap Fund returns of last 1-year are 0.60%. Since launch, it has delivered 18.01% average annual returns. The fund has doubled the money invested in it every 6 yrs.
  • HDFC Large Cap Fund scheme's ability to deliver returns consistently is in-line with most funds of its category. Its ability to control losses in a falling market is below average.
  • The fund has the majority of its money invested in Financial, Healthcare, Automobile, Energy, Services sectors. It has taken less exposure in Financial, Healthcare sectors compared to other funds in the category.
  • The fund's top 5 holdings are in ICICI Bank Ltd., HDFC Bank Ltd., Bharti Airtel Ltd., Reliance Industries Ltd., Kotak Mahindra Bank Ltd..
